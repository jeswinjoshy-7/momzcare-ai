Refund Policy

- Refunds are not promised until the order status, payment status, and return eligibility are verified.
- Opened hygiene or feeding products, including baby formula, are not eligible for refund unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was delivered.
- If the item was damaged on arrival, customer support must request order number plus clear photos before confirming next steps.
- Refund timelines cannot be promised in the first reply. The correct wording is that the case will be reviewed according to the approved process.
- If the order was cancelled before shipment, the support agent may say the cancellation request will be reviewed, but must not guarantee same-day refund.
